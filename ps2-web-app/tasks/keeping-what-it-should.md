# Keeping what it should

Follow-up questions on Problem Set 2, about your own app and how you built it. Answer each one in
one to three sentences. Most of them have no single right answer: what earns credit is being
specific about what actually happened.

### q-describe-your-app

Describe your PS2 app in one sentence. Then say whether it has some kind of admin interface, or
whether every feature is meant to be used by every user.


### q-fix-after-first-round

Describe one element of your PS2 app that did not come out the way you intended on the first round
of implementation. How did you get the agent to fix it?


### q-review-spec-or-plan

Superpowers writes two files before it builds anything: a spec and a plan. Which is more important
for you to review: the spec, the plan, both equally, or neither? Say why.


### q-how-finished

How did you decide your PS2 app was finished?
