# Reflection

**Write these by hand.** Not your agent, not a draft from your agent that you tidied up.
`INSTRUCTIONS.md` says why, and the reason is about your quiz score rather than your conscience:
the quiz reads this file and builds you a question out of it.

**A few sentences each. There are no marks for length.** Four of the five ask you to name
something you rejected, something that broke, or somewhere you were sloppy, and long answers to
those are usually evasions.

**Note your balance at `toolkit.umgpt.umich.edu` before you start**, the way you did in both
labs. Question 5 needs it and you cannot get it back afterwards.

**What a non-answer looks like**, so you can tell when you have written one:

> *I learned a lot about balancing user experience with technical constraints, and I made sure
> to carefully consider all the options before deciding on the best approach for my app.*

Nothing in that names a thing. Every question below asks for a specific: an alternative, a
number, a bug, a moment.
